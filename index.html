Our Heritage

NutriKitchen is a Palakkad, Kerala–based food venture dedicated to bringing traditional, wholesome nutrition back to everyday life. Rooted in the agricultural heritage of our region, NutriKitchen began with a simple idea: to make it easy for modern families to enjoy the goodness of indigenous grains and cold-pressed oils in their daily diet.

Who We Are

NutriKitchen is a home-grown brand built by people who understand farming, food, and family health. Our team works closely with local growers and small producers to source clean, responsibly grown ingredients. Every product and recipe is developed to balance authentic taste with real nutritional value.

What We Make

Our signature range is a collection of millet-based health mixes crafted for all age groups. These mixes are designed to be easy to prepare while retaining the natural fibre, vitamins, and minerals of millets.

Alongside our health mixes, we also offer cold-pressed coconut oil, sesame oil, and groundnut oil, produced to preserve natural aroma, flavour, and nutrients. In addition, we supply quality paddy seeds, reflecting our deep connection with farmers and the cultivation side of food.

NutriKitchen Restaurant

To bring our philosophy directly to the plate, we run the NutriKitchen restaurant, where customers can experience how tasty healthy food can be. The menu highlights millet dishes, traditional Kerala recipes, and meals prepared using our own cold-pressed oils.

It serves as a live showcase of how NutriKitchen products fit seamlessly into everyday cooking, making healthy eating feel natural—not like a compromise.

Our Philosophy

NutriKitchen stands for honest ingredients, mindful processing, and recipes that respect both tradition and modern lifestyles. The focus is on minimal processing, clean labels, and transparency—from where we source to how we make.

"Whether you are cooking at home with our products or dining at our restaurant, NutriKitchen aims to make nutritious food enjoyable, accessible, and part of your daily routine."
